# Qflow Consulting — LinkedIn Company Page Setup Guide

## 1. Create the page
LinkedIn → Work icon (top right) → **Create a Company Page** → **Company**.
Note: only a personal LinkedIn profile can create/admin a Company Page (LinkedIn requires this — no way around it). Use the founder's/your profile as the first admin.

## 2. Page identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Page name | **Qflow Consulting** |
| LinkedIn URL | linkedin.com/company/qflow-consulting (check availability; fallback: qflow-consulting-uae) |
| Industry | Business Consulting and Services |
| Company size | 2–10 employees (adjust to reality) |
| Company type | Privately Held |
| Website | your domain, e.g. www.qflowconsulting.com |
| Headquarters | Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates |
| Tagline (120 char max) | **"ISO & Quality Management Consulting — Get Audit-Ready, Stay Compliant."** |

Alternative taglines (pick one):
- "Your ISO 9001 & 27001 Audit-Readiness Partner in the UAE"
- "Quality Management Systems That Pass Audits — and Actually Work"

## 3. About section (2,600 char max)

**Short version (for the summary card):**
Qflow Consulting helps UAE-based organizations build, audit-proof, and maintain their Quality and Information Security Management Systems. We specialize in ISO 9001, ISO 27001, internal audit programs, CAPA, and documentation control — turning compliance from a burden into a business advantage.

## 4. Visual assets
- **Logo**: 300×300 px, square, transparent PNG, simple mark that reads at 32px (favicon size)
- **Cover banner**: 1128×191 px — tagline + subtle process/checklist motif, brand colors
- Keep a consistent color palette (suggest: deep navy + teal/green accent — signals trust + "quality/compliance")

## 5. Buttons & settings
- CTA button: **"Contact us"** → link to a booking page or WhatsApp/email
- Add **Locations**: Abu Dhabi, UAE (add others if you operate in other Emirates)
- Turn on **Notify employees** when the page publishes new posts (once you have teammates on LinkedIn)
- Add 3–5 **Hashtags the Page follows/uses**: #ISO9001 #ISO27001 #QualityManagement #InternalAudit #AuditReadiness #CAPA #UAEBusiness

## 6. First 3 actions after publishing
1. Post the "Day 1" launch post (see 14-day content calendar) immediately — a page with zero posts looks abandoned
2. Invite your personal network + past/current clients to follow
3. Turn on **Page Admin** for a second trusted person (Super Admin), so the page isn't a single point of failure

## 7. Posting cadence after the 14-day sprint
- 3×/week is sustainable long-term (Mon/Wed/Thu works well for B2B UAE audiences, late morning 9:30–11:30 GST)
- Mix: educational post → tool/template → light case story/testimonial → repeat
